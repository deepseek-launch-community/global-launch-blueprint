# Project: Adaptive-K Routing Collaboration
*A community-driven initiative to explore and advance the Adaptive-K Routing proposal for MoE models.*

## 🎯 Mission
To collaborate closely with the proposal author **[@Gabrobals](https://github.com/Gabrobals)** and the broader community to:
1.  Deepen the technical understanding of the Adaptive-K Routing mechanism.
2.  Explore its integration feasibility and benefits for models like DeepSeek-V3.
3.  Produce tangible outputs (analysis, benchmarks, prototypes) valuable to the open-source ecosystem.
