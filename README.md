# DeepSeek Global Launch Community

> 🌟 **A Community Hub for DeepSeek Builders & Thinkers**

Welcome to our independent, community-driven space. We are not the official DeepSeek team, but a collective of enthusiasts, developers, and practitioners.
我们并非官方团队，而是一群 DeepSeek 的深度爱好者、开发者和实践者。

We focus on **connections and creations “beyond the code”**, aiming to be a vibrant complement to the official ecosystem.
我们专注于 **“代码之外”的联结与创造**，致力于成为官方生态的活力补充。

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

We exist to connect people inspired by DeepSeek‘s technology. Here, we:
我们的存在，是为了连接那些同样被 DeepSeek 技术所激励的人们。在这里，我们：

- **Share Stories**: Document the “human” elements behind every contribution and idea.
- **分享故事**：记录每一次贡献、探索和创意背后的“人”与思考。
- **Spark Inspiration**: Inspire each other through real project showcases and deep dives.
- **激发灵感**：通过真实的项目展示与技术探讨，相互启发，共同成长。
- **Help Each Other**: Foster a friendly space where every practical question finds an answer.
- **互助解惑**：建立一个友好、专业的氛围，让任何问题都能得到倾听与解答。

---

## 🗺️ Our Role

| Official Repos (e.g., DeepSeek-Coder) | **This Community** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Focus**: Code, models, Issues & PRs | **Focus**: People, stories, ideas & applications |
| **Handles**: Concrete bugs, fixes, feature requests | **Incubates**: Early-stage ideas, collaborations, experiences |
| **Process**: Structured, formal, asynchronous | **Vibe**: Open, collaborative, conversational |
| **Goal**: Maintain and improve the projects | **Goal**: Connect and empower each individual |

**Simply put: For specific code changes, go to the official repos. To share stories, discuss ideas, or showcase work, this is your home.**
**简单来说：如果你有明确的代码要提交，请去官方仓库；如果你想分享故事、讨论想法或展示作品，这里就是你的家。**

- **Improve Discussions**: Enrich conversations with your knowledge.

- **Suggest Ideas**: Start a discussion about how we can do better.

- **Spread Kindness**: Maintain a friendly atmosphere per our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
