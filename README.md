
# DeepSeek Global Launch Community

Welcome to the **DeepSeek Global Launch Community**! We are an open collective of developers, researchers, and enthusiasts focused on frontier exploration, technical interpretation, and efficiency innovation within the DeepSeek ecosystem.

## 🎯 Our Mission
To serve as a dynamic **"Frontier Tech Observatory"** and **"Collaboration Sandbox"** within the DeepSeek ecosystem, connecting ideas with implementation and providing a platform for deep engagement and value creation.

## 🔥 Trending Topics in Our Community
Our research evolves dynamically in [Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions). Instead of a fixed roadmap, here’s what the community is actively exploring right now:

- 🧠 **SRCP Series (Structured Reasoning & Compliance Protocol)** : A deep dive into theoretical foundations, validation protocols, and engineering demos.
- 🎨 **Project Showcase**: See what community members are building with DeepSeek models and share your own creations.
- 💡 **Ideas & Proposals**: Open floor for proposing new research directions or collaboration formats.

**👉 [Explore All Active Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions)**

## 🤝 How to Participate
We champion an **asynchronous, deep, written** collaboration culture. You contribute on your own schedule.

**📍 First Stop for Newcomers:**
1.  **Check the [Glossary of Terms](./docs/GLOSSARY.md)** : Understand key concepts like "MoE," "SRCP," or "Adaptive-K" to catch up on the context quickly.
2.  **Browse Discussions**: Head to the [Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions) tab and find a topic that sparks your interest. The "General" and "Ideas" categories are great places to start.
3.  **Join the Conversation**: Drop a comment, share a paper, or just observe. No check-ins required—just clear logic and public contribution records.

**Collaboration Principles**: No fixed hours, no real-time pressure. We value thoughtful written contributions that serve as a valuable addition to your technical portfolio.

# DeepSeek 全球发布社区

欢迎来到 **DeepSeek 全球发布社区**！我们是一个由开发者、研究者和技术爱好者组成的开放社区，专注于 **DeepSeek 模型生态的前沿探索、技术解读与效率创新**。

## 🎯 社区使命
成为 DeepSeek 生态中一个活跃的 **“前沿技术望远镜”** 与 **“协作创新沙盒”**，连接创意与实现，为开发者提供深度参与和创造价值的平台。

## 🔥 社区热门话题
我们的研究项目在 [Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions) 中动态演进。以下是当前社区成员正在积极探讨的话题：

- 🧠 **SRCP 系列研究 (结构化推理与合规协议)**：对理论基础、验证协议及工程演示的深度探讨。
- 🎨 **作品秀 (Project Showcase)**：围观社区成员基于 DeepSeek 构建的作品，也欢迎分享你的创作。
- 💡 **新想法与提案**：如果你有新的研究方向或协作点子，这里是你发起的舞台。

**👉 [查看所有活跃讨论](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions)**

## 🤝 如何参与
我们倡导 **异步、深度、书面化** 的协作文化，让你可以自由安排时间贡献智慧。

**📍 给新朋友的第一站：**
1.  **查看 [技术术语表](./docs/GLOSSARY.md)**：快速理解 “MoE”、“SRCP” 或 “Adaptive-K” 等讨论语境中的关键概念。
2.  **浏览讨论区**：前往 [Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions) 板块，寻找你感兴趣的话题。建议从 “General” 或 “Ideas” 分类开始。
3.  **加入对话**：留下评论、分享论文，或仅仅是保持关注。这里无打卡要求，我们只重视清晰的逻辑与公开的贡献记录。

**协作原则**：无坐班、无即时响应压力。我们珍视深思熟虑的书面交流，这将是您技术履历中宝贵的佐证。
