# DeepSeek Global Launch Community

Welcome to the **DeepSeek Global Launch Community**! We are an open community of developers, researchers, and enthusiasts, focused on **frontier exploration, technical interpretation, and efficiency innovation within the DeepSeek model ecosystem**.

## 🎯 Our Mission
To become a vibrant **"Frontier Tech Observatory"** and **"Collaboration Sandbox"** within the DeepSeek ecosystem, connecting ideas with implementation, and providing a platform for developers to engage deeply and create value.

## 🔥 Active Core Project

### 【Forward-Looking Study】DeepSeek-MoE + Adaptive-K Routing Integration Assessment
We are conducting an in-depth study to theoretically analyze the feasibility, potential, and challenges of integrating Adaptive-K dynamic routing with the DeepSeek-MoE architecture.

- **Nature**: Open, asynchronous, document-driven **forward-looking research**.
- **Goals**: Architecture mapping analysis, theoretical benefit estimation report, draft integration roadmap.
- **How to Join**: Open to all interested researchers, architects, and documentation specialists.
- **Join the Discussion**: [Enter the Async Project Kick-off](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions/6#discussioncomment-15613926)

## 🤝 How to Participate
We champion an **asynchronous, deep, written** collaboration culture, allowing you to contribute on your own schedule:
1.  **Browse & Join Discussions**: Go to [Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions) to find topics of interest.
2.  **Claim Research Tasks**: Within the core project, choose your area (architecture, modeling, documentation) to contribute.
3.  **Propose New Ideas**: Feel free to start a new discussion for a new research topic or collaboration idea.

**Collaboration Principles**: No fixed hours, no check-ins, no real-time response required. We value clear logic and public contribution records, which serve as valuable proof for your technical portfolio.

# DeepSeek 全球发布社区

欢迎来到 **DeepSeek 全球发布社区**！我们是一个由开发者、研究者和技术爱好者组成的开放社区，专注于 **DeepSeek 模型生态的前沿探索、技术解读与效率创新**。

## 🎯 社区使命
成为 DeepSeek 生态中一个活跃的 **“前沿技术望远镜”** 与 **“协作创新沙盒”**，连接创意与实现，为开发者提供深度参与和创造价值的平台。

## 🔥 核心进行中项目

### 【前瞻性研究】DeepSeek-MoE 与 Adaptive-K 路由集成方案评估
我们正在开展一项深度研究，旨在从理论层面分析 Adaptive-K 动态路由技术与 DeepSeek-MoE 架构融合的可行性、潜力与挑战。

- **项目性质**：开放式、异步协作、文档驱动的**前瞻性研究**。
- **目标产出**：架构映射分析、理论收益推演报告、集成路线图草案等。
- **参与方式**：欢迎所有对此感兴趣的研究者、架构师和文档高手加入。
- **立即加入讨论**： [点击进入项目异步启动会](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions/6#discussioncomment-15613926)

## 🤝 如何参与
我们倡导 **异步、深度、书面化** 的协作文化，让你可以自由安排时间贡献智慧：
1.  **浏览并加入讨论**：前往 [Discussions](https://github.com/deepseek-launch-community/global-launch-blueprint/discussions) 寻找你感兴趣的话题。
2.  **认领研究任务**：在上述核心项目中，选择你擅长的方向（架构、建模、文档）参与。
3.  **提出新想法**：如果你有新的研究主题或协作点子，欢迎发起新的讨论。

**协作原则**：无坐班、无打卡、无即时响应要求。我们重视清晰的逻辑与公开的贡献记录，这将是你技术履历的宝贵证明。
